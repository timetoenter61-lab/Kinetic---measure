Hello and Welcome

Able Scales was established in 2004 in Brisbane, Queensland, providing commercial and retail sales, service, and hire throughout metropolitan and surrounding Brisbane areas. The business was purchased in 2023 and has since upgraded equipment and staff to include a specialist heavy-capacity weighing division.

Our team includes members with over 30 years of heavy-capacity weighing experience. We are now based in Dalby, Queensland, and service regional Queensland and New South Wales.

We are a mobile team and support clients where they operate. We come to your site for consultations, setup, and practical guidance so your system performs properly in real conditions. We also support local innovation through startup product development partnerships.
