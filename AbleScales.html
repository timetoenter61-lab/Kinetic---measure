Reliable Field Support Across Regional Queensland and NSW

Our team works across regional Queensland and New South Wales, providing support and advice in industrial electronics, web monitoring, app design, and rural technology solutions. We supply Queensland-made Razorback instruments and weighing systems.

Our team is mobile and on the road constantly, supporting clients where they operate. We come to your location for consultations, setup, and practical guidance so your system performs properly in real conditions.

We are based in Dalby, Queensland, and focus on regional clients with a direct, practical approach.
